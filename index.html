How it works

From download to playing in three steps

No dependencies to hunt down, no config files to hand-edit.

1

Point modvault at your games

On first launch, modvault auto-detects supported game installs. Confirm the folders, or add any game manually — it remembers every path per profile.

2

Load, order and resolve conflicts

Hit enable and modvault stages everything into place. Swap mods mid-session, export the setup as a modpack, or script the whole flow via CLI.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you might want to know before installing modvault.

Which games does modvault support?+
modvault is game-agnostic. It ships with built-in profiles for popular modding titles and lets you register any game folder manually, so the load-order, conflict-resolution and modpack tools work across your whole library.
Is it really free and open source?+
Yes. modvault is free to download and use, and the full source is available on GitHub. There are no premium tiers, no mod-count limits and no telemetry — just the universal mod manager and its CLI.
Will hot-swapping break my game saves?+
modvault never overwrites your originals — it stages changes and virtualizes the game directory. If you toggle a mod off, the staged files are reverted instantly. As always, keep a backup of important saves before changing load orders mid-playthrough.
